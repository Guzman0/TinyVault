Deploy TinyVault

Deploys the Wave 2 vault on Mezo testnet from your wallet, then opens its shared Mezo loan. Your wallet signs everything; nothing else holds your keys.

1Connect

Not connected.

2Deploy the vault

Your wallet becomes the owner. Settings: borrow up to 150%, close below 125%, 5% closer bonus, 10 MUSD smallest loan, 0.5% reserve fee.

3Open the shared Mezo loan

Borrows Mezo's 1,800 MUSD minimum against your BTC. You receive the 1,800 MUSD. Keep a little BTC for gas.
